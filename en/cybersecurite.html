Cybersecurity

Protect your organization from cybercriminals

Ransomware, phishing, data theft: cyberattacks do not only target large companies — any organization that depends on its IT systems is a potential target. KenTeKoD helps you build continuous vigilance across your portfolios, projects and programs: security audits, incident tracking, IT asset inventory and risk governance, on infrastructure designed for Canadian hosting.

Why it matters

Your organization's security is something you manage, not something you catch up on afterward

A forgotten security audit, an unrecorded incident, an IT asset whose update status nobody knows — each of these blind spots is an entry point for a cybercriminal. Most breaches are not caused by a sophisticated attack, but by tracking that slipped somewhere.

Continuous vigilance, not a one-off audit

What you need to build a solid organizational security posture

Tracked security audits

Plan, track and document your organization's security audits — findings, remediation and status, in the same place as the rest of your management.

Security incident management

Record, prioritize and track the resolution of any incident — intrusion attempt, suspicious behaviour, suspected outage — as soon as it occurs.

IT asset inventory

Keep an up-to-date view of all your technology assets — an essential prerequisite: you cannot protect what you do not know you own.

Technology interventions

Document the security patches, updates and infrastructure changes applied to reduce your attack surface.

Our own standard

We apply to our own infrastructure the protection principles we recommend for your organization

Hosting in Canada

Microsoft Entra ID authentication

Modern cloud architecture

Built on managed Azure services, with data encryption, offering enterprise-grade availability and resilience.
