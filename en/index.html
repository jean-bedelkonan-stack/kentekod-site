What KENTEKOD does

One tool for your portfolios, projects and programs

Every module draws on the same data — no re-entry, no scattered Excel files — built for the reality of teams steering initiatives at every scale, from the strategic portfolio to the delivery of each project.

Planning

Gantt chart, work breakdown structure (WBS) and automatic scheduling that respects your working calendar.

Resources

Management of human, material and cost resources — named or generic profiles, depending on your needs.

Finance

Invoicing, purchase orders and CAPEX/OPEX budget tracking, with automatic intake of invoices received by email.

Risk & compliance

Risk register, major incidents and change requests, tracked in the same place as your projects.
